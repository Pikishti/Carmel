How It Started

Made in Small Batches, By Hand

CARMEL Goods began in a small kitchen with a simple idea: the best things in life are the ones you take the time to enjoy. What started as syrups and butters made for friends and family slowly grew into a small-batch brand built around one belief — that joy lives in life's simplest moments, and it's worth slowing down for.

Every bottle and jar is still made by hand, in small batches, with the same care as that very first batch.

What We Believe

Simple Moments, Fully Lived

Inspired by the art of savoring life, Carmel was created for those who find joy in life's simplest moments and choose to live it fully. A slow cup of coffee. A warm plate of waffles. Bread, fresh from the oven, with butter melting into every corner. These are the moments we make for.

We believe good ingredients and unhurried care make all the difference — so that's how we make everything, one small batch at a time.
